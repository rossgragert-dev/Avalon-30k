# v0.1 Playable Slice — The Nameless Road

> Working title and conversion milestone.

## Player fantasy

The player wakes with no usable personal history. They remember language, ordinary knowledge, and the practical skills represented by one chosen class, but not the identity that once connected those skills to a life.

The engine uses **Wanderer** as a temporary safe character handle. It is not intended to be the final canonical name.

## First playable loop

1. Launch the game.
2. Choose **New Game**.
3. Read the unknown-origin opening.
4. Choose one of the nine inherited classes.
5. See a class-specific first memory fragment.
6. Enter the inherited open world as temporary test scenery.
7. Fight, explore, quest, loot, equip, level, and use the inherited class kit.
8. Leave or background the game.
9. Return and choose **Continue**.
10. Restore the same class and serialized character state.

## Classes retained

- Warrior
- Paladin
- Hunter
- Rogue
- Priest
- Shaman
- Mage
- Warlock
- Druid

These are currently inherited mechanical identities. Later milestones can rename or reshape individual classes only if doing so improves the new setting without discarding useful gameplay depth.

## Narrative seed

The known world is divided among successor realms built over the remains of a much older dominion. The old civilization left roads, fortresses, libraries, magical works, and sealed places that later peoples only partly understand.

Something is changing:

- old roads are becoming dangerous;
- creatures are moving out of familiar territories;
- people disappear near ancient sites;
- strange phenomena return to structures dormant for centuries;
- rival kingdoms interpret the crisis differently;
- a recurring symbol appears in both the ancient mystery and the protagonist's lost history.

The protagonist is not announced as a chosen savior. Their importance should emerge through accumulated choices, relationships, discoveries, titles, and consequences.

## Replay philosophy

The goal is not nine totally separate campaigns. Most geography and major events should be shared, while class, faction, companion, and identity systems alter how the player understands and resolves them.

A later target is approximately **60–70% shared world/content and 30–40% class-, faction-, or choice-sensitive material**.

Examples:

- Warrior: leadership, armies, oaths, kingdoms, command.
- Mage: ancient knowledge, magical institutions, forbidden research.
- Rogue: courts, spies, criminal networks, infiltration.
- Priest: faith, institutions, miracles, competing interpretations.
- Warlock: dangerous knowledge and forces willing to answer back.
- Hunter/Druid/Shaman: threats and truths visible first beyond settled lands.
- Paladin: duty, authority, orders, and the cost of keeping an oath.

## Not in this slice

- final game title;
- final map;
- final opening region;
- final factions or kingdom names;
- final ancient-civilization terminology;
- class-specific main questlines;
- identity-reveal system;
- multiple endings;
- companion system conversion;
- full removal of online/server/live-service code;
- replacement of all inherited media assets.

The slice exists to prove that the class-driven, persistent single-player foundation works before large content production begins.
